<h2 id="react-form-vs-react-hook-form">React Form vs React-Hook-Form<a class="heading-anchor" aria-hidden="true" tabindex="-1" href="#react-form-vs-react-hook-form"><span class="icon icon-link"></span></a></h2>
<h4 id="introduction-to-react-hook-form-and-its-advantages">Introduction to react-hook-form and its advantages<a class="heading-anchor" aria-hidden="true" tabindex="-1" href="#introduction-to-react-hook-form-and-its-advantages"><span class="icon icon-link"></span></a></h4>
<p>A library that makes building forms in React easy. As stated in the <a href="https://react-hook-form.com/">official docs</a>, it is performant, flexible, and excellent at validation.</p>
<h4 id="advantages">Advantages<a class="heading-anchor" aria-hidden="true" tabindex="-1" href="#advantages"><span class="icon icon-link"></span></a></h4>
<ul>
<li>less code, easier inputs</li>
<li>don’t deal with events</li>
<li>better validation
<ul>
<li>Controlled at the JavaScript layer rather than through HTML attributes
<ul>
<li>Can defend against malicious attacks (if HTML attributes are modified, the browser fails to recognize them: required, minLength, etc.)</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>better error</li>
<li>have control over inputs</li>
<li>Supports TS out of the box</li>
<li>Uses functional components (Hook API)</li>
</ul>
